Our Story

Built for the Business Owner
Who Can't Miss a Call

Missed Calls to Cash is a division of TruePath Development Group, based in Ellicott City, Maryland. We exist to help local service businesses stop losing revenue to unanswered phones.

Our Mission

Every Missed Call Is a
Missed Opportunity

We started Missed Calls to Cash after watching great contractors, plumbers, HVAC technicians, and other tradespeople lose jobs simply because they couldn't answer the phone while working.

The problem isn't work ethic — it's physics. You can't be on a roof and on the phone at the same time. We built a solution so you never have to choose.

What We Believe

Our Core Values

Everything we build is grounded in a simple belief — local businesses deserve enterprise-level tools without enterprise complexity.

01

Tradespeople First

We design for the person on a job site, not the person at a desk. Our tools work the way you work — fast, reliable, and out of the way.

02

No Fluff, Real Results

We measure success in booked jobs and recovered leads — not dashboards nobody reads. If it doesn't help you win more business, we don't ship it.

03

Always On Your Side

You're running a business, not a tech project. We handle the setup, the maintenance, and the support — so you can stay focused on the work that pays.
