Absolutely! We'd love to help with your color. Let me grab your name and a preferred time and I'll get that scheduled for you.

Yes, we do wig installs — frontal, full lace, and closure wigs. Would you like to book an install appointment or ask about preparation?

Yes! Saturday hours are [hours]. Slots fill up fast on weekends — want me to check what's still available this Saturday for you?

Of course! What's your name and current appointment date? I'll find the best available time to move you to. 📅
