Real Questions Clients Ask

The AI Handles All of These

Your AI assistant knows your packages, availability, and policies — so it can answer every inquiry, day or night.

"Are you available for a wedding in June?"
Hi! I'd love to check on June availability for you. Could you share the exact date and venue location so I can confirm? 🎶
"What do you charge for a 4-hour reception?"
Wedding reception packages start at $[price] for up to 4 hours and include setup, sound, and lighting. Want me to send you full package details?
"Do you do corporate events?"
Absolutely! Corporate events are a specialty — from networking mixers to awards dinners. Want to tell me more about your event so I can put together the right package?
"Can you travel to [venue name]?"
Yes, travel is available within [radius] miles and to most major venues in the area. What's the event location? I'll confirm it's in range. 📍
"Do you take song requests?"
Yes! Clients submit must-play and do-not-play lists in advance, and we welcome requests throughout the event when the floor allows. It's your night! 🎵
"What's included in a wedding package?"
Wedding packages include ceremony music, cocktail hour, reception DJ, MC services, sound system, and uplighting. Want me to book a quick consultation call to walk through everything?
"How far in advance do I need to book?"
Wedding dates book 6–12 months out, especially peak season weekends. For private parties and corporate, 4–8 weeks is typical. Want to check your date now?
"Do you provide lighting?"
Yes — uplighting, dance floor lighting, and wireless options are available as add-ons or included in select packages. I can include a lighting quote with your package info!
