What We Do

Home Improvement Services You Can Count On

From decks and porches to interior remodeling and general repairs, Reyes Construction handles a wide range of residential projects for homeowners in Maryland.

Home Improvement

General home improvement services for Maryland homeowners. We help you identify what needs attention and get it done properly the first time.

Deck Repairs & Rebuilds

Worn-out or unsafe deck? We repair, restore, or rebuild wood and composite decks to make them safe, solid, and looking great again.

Porch Repairs

We repair rotted boards, damaged railings, worn steps, and structural issues on porches of all sizes — restoring both function and curb appeal.

Exterior Repairs

Siding damage, fascia, trim, steps, and exterior wood repairs. We protect your home's exterior from Maryland's weather before problems get worse.

Interior Remodeling

Kitchen updates, bathroom renovations, room modifications, and interior improvements that add real value and comfort to your living space.

Carpentry

Custom woodwork, trim installation, framing, doors, windows, and finish carpentry that brings craftsmanship and detail to your home.

General Construction

Additions, structural work, and general construction projects for residential properties. We tackle both large and small-scale builds.

Property Repairs

From small fixes to larger repair projects, we help maintain and protect your property's value with honest assessments and reliable workmanship.
