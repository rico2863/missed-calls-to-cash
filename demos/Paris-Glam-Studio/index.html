What's the difference between a silk press and a blowout?
Great question! A silk press uses flat iron heat after washing to create a super smooth, silky-straight finish with no chemicals. A blowout uses heat from a blow dryer only. The silk press typically lasts longer and gives a more polished result. Would you like to book one? ✨
