What We Do

Exterior Work You Can Count On

From full siding installations to small trim repairs, Sibilia Siding handles exterior projects with care and attention to detail.

🏠

Siding Repair & Installation

Full siding installations and targeted repairs. Vinyl, fiber cement, cedar, and more. Proper prep, clean lines, and a finish that holds up.

🔨

Soffit & Fascia Work

Soffit and fascia protect your roofline and attic ventilation. We replace rotted boards, install new panels, and seal the eaves correctly.

✍

Exterior Trim

Window surrounds, corner boards, door casings, and decorative trim installed clean and painted or primed for a polished exterior finish.

⛈

Storm & Weather Damage Repairs

Wind-blown panels, water-damaged boards, cracked siding from impact — we assess the damage and restore the exterior properly.

🏗

Exterior Remodeling Support

Changing the look of your home's exterior? We work alongside your renovation to handle the siding, soffit, and trim portions of the project.

🔧

Small Exterior Projects & Upgrades

Not everything is a big job. We handle smaller repairs, accent trim, and targeted upgrades that make a real difference in curb appeal.
